# EBaC (Electronic Bible and Concordance)

A fast, keyboard-driven Bible reader and concordance, in a terminal in your Home Assistant sidebar.

- **Read** any passage by book, chapter and verse, and page through it a verse, a screen, or a book at a time.
- **Search** for any word and see every verse that contains it, highlighted, then open any verse in context.
- **Combine** words and books: verses with "love" and "hate" together, "amen" in Revelation, "blessed" in the four Gospels, or words within a few verses of each other.

Includes the King James (Authorized) Version. Written by Neil G. Brookins.
